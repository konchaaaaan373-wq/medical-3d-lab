The asset also contains imaging-atlas-derived deep nuclei and white-matter
structures documented by Brain Project. Those structures remain CC BY-SA 4.0.

gross-anatomy education rather than diagnosis, measurement, or surgical
navigation. See the upstream README and registration notes for the complete

The application does not alter the distributed GLB geometry. It assigns its own

retain the attribution above.
