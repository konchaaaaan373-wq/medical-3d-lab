## 1. What question this model answers

**What is behind what, in an eye?**

## 2. What it is

A **right eye**. Three coats — **sclera**, **choroid**, **retina** — drawn as
shells with walls rather than as nested balls, so a section shows three layers.
The **cornea** is a steeper dome set into the front of the sclera at the limbus.
Behind it the **anterior chamber**, then the **iris** with the **pupil** in the
middle of it, then the **lens**, hung from the **ciliary body**. The
**vitreous body** fills everything behind that. On the back wall the **optic
disc** — nasal — and the **macula** with its fovea, temporal to it; the **optic
nerve** leaves at the disc. Four **rectus muscles** run from behind the eye to
the sclera in front of its widest point. **17 structures are selectable.**

## 3. What it is not

- **Nothing moves.** No accommodation, no pupillary reflex, no eye movement,
  and **intraocular pressure is not represented in any form**.
- **The three coats are drawn far thicker than they are.** In life they are
  fractions of a millimetre against a globe of about 24 mm. It is a display
  thickness so that each can be seen and clicked, named as such in the builder
  (`COAT_DISPLAY_THICKNESS`), and **no thickness may be read off this model**.
- **No radius, angle or distance is a measurement.**
- **The aqueous route is described, not modelled.** The posterior chamber and
  the trabecular meshwork are not drawn; the copy says where the fluid is made,
  where it passes and where it leaves, and the geometry shows only the angle.
- **Not present:** the retinal layers and vessels, the optic cup, the
  suspensory fibres of the lens, the oblique muscles, the eyelids, conjunctiva
  and lacrimal apparatus, the orbit.
- **No disease**, and not anyone's eye.

## 4. Sources and licence

Generated by this repository. No external asset, no imaging, no patient data.

## 5. Accuracy and uncertainty

What the model claims is **the order of things along the axis and the relation
of the two fundus landmarks**, and that is what the tests fix: the three coats
nest outside-in with a gap between each; the cornea stands proud of the globe
while being narrower than it, and meets it at the limbus; cornea, chamber, iris
and pupil, lens and vitreous come in that order; the lens is wider than the
pupil it is seen through and the ciliary ring lies outside its edge; the optic
disc is nasal to the macula and the macula is the one at the posterior pole;
the nerve leaves at the disc and runs back and nasally; and each rectus reaches
the sclera from its own side, from behind, inserting in front of the equator.

## 6. Presentation choices

- **The slider fades the coats inwards**, so the inside is reached without
  anything being taken out of the eye.
- **The section cuts the temporal half away**, leaving the half the optic disc
  is on — a nasal structure does not survive a cut made the other way round.
- **"The fundus" hides the front of the eye by tag.** That is what an
  ophthalmoscope does with light, and it puts the disc and the macula exactly
  where a fundus photograph has them.
- **A click on the front of the eye selects the cornea**, because the cornea is
  what is in front — it is a structure and not a coating, even at the
  transparency it is drawn with. The iris and the lens behind it are reached
  from the parts list, from isolation, or from the section.
- **The pupil is drawn as a black disc**, because an opening cannot be clicked
  and black is what an unlit eye interior looks like. The copy says it is an
  opening and not a structure.
- **Screen-left is the patient's right** (`docs/architecture-rules.md` rule 5),
  so in this right eye nasal is `+x`; every side comes from one `NASAL`
  constant.
- **The frame follows the viewpoint.** The camera fits the organ into the part
  of the window nothing is covering — the parts panel is an overlay over the
  canvas, not a narrower canvas — and a viewpoint that hides a side or cuts the
  organ open is framed on what it leaves rather than on the whole model.
- **A cut draws the face it leaves, where there is one to draw.** For a solid
  part the cross-section is computed from the triangles the plane crosses and
  drawn in that part's own colour, so a cut segment reads as tissue with a
  surface rather than as a shell seen from the inside. For a hollow one — a
  stomach, a duct, a loop of bowel — the cut opens it instead: the wall here is
  a surface with no thickness, and facing it would draw a lumen as a lump of
  tissue. Which a part is, is declared by the scene rather than guessed.

## 7. What it must never be used for

Diagnosis, treatment selection, dose selection, prognosis, or planning any
procedure — injection, laser or surgery included. It carries no pressure, no
acuity and no threshold.
