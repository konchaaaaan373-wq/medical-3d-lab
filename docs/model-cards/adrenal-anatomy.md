## 1. What question this model answers

**Why is one gland made of four layers that do four unrelated things?**

## 2. What it is

Each gland is cut into four concentric shells: **zona glomerulosa**, **zona
fasciculata**, **zona reticularis** and the **medulla** — salt, sugar, sex, then
catecholamines, from the capsule inwards. The right gland is pyramidal and caps
its kidney; the left is crescentic and leans on the medial border of its own.
Both **kidneys** are drawn, because that difference is what the two shapes are
for. **10 structures are selectable.**

The slider fades the layers outside in, one at a time, which is the only order
in which four nested shells can be read.

## 3. What it is not

- **The zone thicknesses are not the real proportions.** They are drawn so that
  three zones can be told apart on a screen. In life the cortex is about nine
  tenths of the gland and the glomerulosa is a thin rim inside its capsule;
  drawn to scale, the three zones would be three lines. **Only their order is
  claimed.**
- **Not measured.** No dimension here is a measurement.
- **Not present:** the capsule, the arterial supply and the single draining
  vein on each side (which differ between the two, and that difference matters
  in venous sampling), the chromaffin cells and their sympathetic supply.
- **No secretion.** Nothing is released and nothing is solved;
  `adrenal-response` is where the two time courses live.

## 4. Sources and licence

Generated by this repository. No external asset, no imaging, no patient data.

## 5. Accuracy and uncertainty

The claim is the **order of the layers** and the **difference between the two
glands**, and both are held in `tests/organ-parts-anatomy.test.js`: each zone
encloses the next, the medulla is the innermost and is enclosed by all three
zones, and the right gland sits on top of its kidney while the left sits beside
its kidney's medial border.

No dimension here is a measurement.

## 6. Presentation choices

- **The layers fade outside in, one at a time.** Four nested shells cannot be
  read in any other order: the outermost has to go before the next one is a
  surface.
- **Every layer is drawn double-sided**, because a shell that has been opened is
  being looked at from inside.
- **The kidneys are context**, excluded from the framing subject. They are drawn
  for one reason and the copy gives it.
- **Colour separates the three zones and the medulla**; "Natural tissue" puts
  cortical colour back and leaves the medulla distinguishable, because it is not
  cortex.

## 7. What it must never be used for

Diagnosis, treatment selection, dose selection, prognosis, or planning any
procedure — adrenalectomy, adrenal venous sampling or biopsy included.
