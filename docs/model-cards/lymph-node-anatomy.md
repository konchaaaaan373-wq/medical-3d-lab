## 1. What question this model answers

**Why can nothing travelling in lymph get past a node?**

## 2. What it is

One lymph node, at its own scale. A **capsule** round a **cortex** with its
**lymphoid follicles**, and a **medulla** reaching towards the **hilum**.
Several **afferent vessels** arrive on the convex surface; a single **efferent
vessel** leaves at the hilum. **7 structures are selectable.**

**This is a companion scene.** The body-scale routes are `lymphatic-drainage`,
and the split is deliberate: a node is millimetres and the thoracic duct is
most of a person, and one model cannot draw both and stay honest.

## 3. What it is not

- **A schematic node, not a magnified one.** The regions are drawn as three
  depths of one outline so each can be seen and clicked. A real node's cortex,
  paracortex and medulla are not even shells.
- **Nothing flows and nothing swells.** That the cortex enlarges first is stated
  as anatomy — where the follicles are — and is not animated.
- **No size, proportion or count is a measurement.** Five afferents are drawn
  because "several" needs a number to draw; the number is not a claim.
- **Not present:** the subcapsular and medullary sinuses, the paracortex,
  germinal centres, the reticular framework, the hilar artery and vein, the
  valves in the vessels.
- **No disease**, and not anyone's node.

## 4. Sources and licence

Generated by this repository. No external asset, no imaging, no patient data.

## 5. Accuracy and uncertainty

What the model claims is **many in, one out, and three depths between them**,
and that is what the tests fix: capsule contains cortex contains medulla; the
follicles lie inside the cortex and there is more than one; there are several
afferents and exactly one efferent; the afferents arrive from outside the
convex side and the efferent leaves past the other; they are at opposite ends,
so lymph passes through; the efferent leaves at the hilum and no afferent
arrives there; and the medulla reaches towards the way out.

## 6. Presentation choices

- **The slider fades the capsule, then the cortex**, so the inside is reached
  without the node being opened.
- **A section view exists** because a node is drawn cut in half everywhere
  else, and it cuts rather than separating the halves.
- **The hilum is a marker as well as a place**, so "where everything leaves"
  can be pointed at without pointing at a vessel.
- **The afferents are one structure**, because which of them a given lymphatic
  is is not a question anybody asks.
- **The frame follows the viewpoint.** The camera fits the organ into the part
  of the window nothing is covering — the parts panel is an overlay over the
  canvas, not a narrower canvas — and a viewpoint that hides a side or cuts the
  organ open is framed on what it leaves rather than on the whole model.
- **A cut draws the face it leaves.** The cross-section is computed from the
  triangles the plane crosses and drawn in each structure's own colour, so a
  cut part reads as solid tissue with a surface rather than as a shell seen
  from the inside. It is the geometry of the model, not an illustration laid
  over it: what the face shows is where the plane happens to fall.

## 7. What it must never be used for

Diagnosis, treatment selection, dose selection, prognosis, staging, or planning
any biopsy or dissection.
