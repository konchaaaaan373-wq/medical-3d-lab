## 1. What question this model answers

**Where does each sinus actually let go of what is in it?**

## 2. What it is

A **right nasal cavity** with the midline structures that bound it. The
**external nose** stands off the face and the **nasal vestibule** is the first
room inside the nostril; the **nasal septum** divides one space into two, the
**lateral nasal wall** carries everything, and the **hard palate** is the floor.
Three shelves — **inferior**, **middle** and **superior turbinate** — hang off
that wall, and under each is the gutter of the same name: **inferior**,
**middle** and **superior meatus**. The **maxillary sinus** opens through the
**maxillary ostium** near its roof into the middle meatus; the **frontal
sinus**, the **ethmoid air cells** and the **sphenoid sinus** are placed against
the same wall. The **nasolacrimal duct** comes down into the inferior meatus,
the **olfactory region** is a patch on the roof with its filaments leaving
upwards, and the **nasopharynx** is where both cavities arrive. **19 structures
are selectable.**

## 3. What it is not

- **Nothing moves.** No air flows, no mucus is cleared, and **nothing swells** —
  the hourly cycle of turbinate swelling the copy describes is not animated.
- **Only one side is drawn.** The left cavity, its turbinates and its sinuses
  are absent. The external nose, septum, palate and nasopharynx are midline or
  shared and are drawn whole.
- **The middle meatus is a plain gutter.** The uncinate process, ethmoid bulla
  and hiatus semilunaris that actually shape the ostiomeatal complex are not
  drawn, so **the arrangement inside it cannot be read off this model** — only
  that three sinuses arrive there.
- **No dimension, angle or sinus volume is a measurement.** That the maxillary
  ostium is near the roof rather than the floor is a fact about the sinus; how
  far above the floor it is drawn is not.
- **No variation.** A deviated septum, an absent frontal sinus, an air-filled
  middle turbinate and the differing ethmoid cell counts are all normal and none
  of them is shown.
- **Not present:** the frontal and ethmoid drainage channels as geometry, the
  sphenoethmoidal recess, the cribriform plate and olfactory bulb, the teeth
  below the maxillary sinus, the orbit, the soft palate, and the openings of the
  Eustachian tubes.
- **No disease**, and not anyone's nose.

## 4. Sources and licence

Generated by this repository. No external asset, no imaging, no patient data.

## 5. Accuracy and uncertainty

What the model claims is **which gutter each thing opens into**, and that is
what the tests fix: the three shelves stack in order on one wall and each one
reaches medially without touching the septum; each gutter lies below the shelf
it is named for and above the next one down; the maxillary ostium leaves the
sinus **well above its floor** and ends inside the middle meatus and nowhere
else; the tear duct ends inside the inferior meatus; the olfactory patch sits on
the roof above every turbinate; and the cavity runs from the nostril back to the
choana with the nasopharynx behind it.

## 6. Presentation choices

- **The cavity is drawn as narrow as it is.** Nothing here is widened to be
  easier to click. What opens it is the **septum stepping back** under the layer
  slider and the viewpoints that come round to the medial side — which is how a
  lateral wall is looked at in life.
- **The three gutters are drawn as the spaces they are**, faint but solid enough
  to be picked, because a space cannot otherwise be pointed at and these three
  are the answer to the scene's question.
- **The coronal view is a real cut**, on the plane this anatomy is imaged in,
  and it is the only view in which the maxillary sinus, its opening and the
  gutter that opening arrives in are visible together.
- **The sinus views hide the lateral wall by tag.** A sheet of bone is exactly
  what is between a reader and a sinus in life; taking it away is a display
  choice, moving the sinus would not be.
- **The ethmoid is one structure with six meshes**, because a honeycomb whose
  cell count differs in everybody is one thing to point at, not six.
- **Screen-left is the patient's right** (`docs/architecture-rules.md` rule 5),
  so in this right cavity medial is `+x`; every side comes from one `MEDIAL`
  constant.
- **One surface, three uses.** `turbinateSurface` is the mid-surface of a
  turbinate; the shelf is built around it, the gutter below takes its roof from
  it and the gutter above takes its floor from it, so they cannot drift apart
  (`docs/architecture-rules.md` rule 1).

## 7. What it must never be used for

Diagnosis, treatment selection, dose selection, prognosis, or planning any
procedure — **including any endoscopic route**, which this model is explicitly
too coarse to support: the structures a route is defined by are the ones §3 says
are not drawn.
