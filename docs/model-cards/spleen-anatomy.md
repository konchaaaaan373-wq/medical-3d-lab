## 1. What question this model answers

**Why can part of a spleen be removed and the rest live?**

## 2. What it is

The spleen's own shape, cut at a transverse plane through the hilum into the
**superior** and **inferior segments** — the territories of the splenic
artery's two terminal branches. The **splenic artery** is drawn dividing
*before* it reaches the hilum, each branch going to one segment, with the
**splenic vein** leaving behind them and the **tail of the pancreas** reaching
the hilum beside them. **7 structures are selectable.**

The slider fades the parenchyma so that where the division comes from can be
seen.

## 3. What it is not

- **The segments are territories of blood supply, not layers of tissue.** Red
  and white pulp are histology and are not drawn.
- **The plane is flat and real ones are not.** A real intersegmental plane is
  curved, and it is not in the same place in two people. Two segments is the
  usual number; three and four occur.
- **Not measured.** Every dimension is schematic.
- **Not present:** capsule and trabeculae as their own structures, short gastric
  and left gastroepiploic branches, the gastrosplenic and splenorenal ligaments,
  accessory spleens, the stomach and left kidney that press on the visceral
  surface.
- **No flow.** Nothing moves and nothing is solved; `spleen-filtration` is where
  that lives.

## 4. Sources and licence

Generated by this repository. No external asset, no imaging, no patient data.

## 5. Accuracy and uncertainty

What the model claims is arrangement: the two segments partition the
parenchyma; the artery divides before the hilum and each branch reaches its own
segment; the vein leaves posterior to the artery. Those are held in
`tests/organ-parts-anatomy.test.js`, and the part division and bilingual naming
through the anatomy contract in `tests/organ-anatomy-scenes.test.js`.

No dimension here is a measurement.

## 6. Presentation choices

- **The parenchyma fades, the vessels appear.** Nothing is moved and nothing is
  removed.
- **A "Visceral surface and hilum" viewpoint exists** because the hilum is a
  recess and cannot be read from the diaphragmatic side; every vessel names it
  as the view it prefers.
- **The pancreatic tail is context**, excluded from the framing subject. It is
  drawn for one reason, and the copy gives it: it is why a splenectomy can
  injure a pancreas.
- **Colour separates the two segments**; "Natural tissue" puts one splenic
  colour back.
- **The frame follows the viewpoint.** The camera fits the organ into the part
  of the window nothing is covering — the parts panel is an overlay over the
  canvas, not a narrower canvas — and a viewpoint that hides a side or cuts the
  organ open is framed on what it leaves rather than on the whole model.
- **A cut draws the face it leaves, where there is one to draw.** For a solid
  part the cross-section is computed from the triangles the plane crosses and
  drawn in that part's own colour, so a cut segment reads as tissue with a
  surface rather than as a shell seen from the inside. For a hollow one — a
  stomach, a duct, a loop of bowel — the cut opens it instead: the wall here is
  a surface with no thickness, and facing it would draw a lumen as a lump of
  tissue. Which a part is, is declared by the scene rather than guessed.
- **Isolating a structure shows it solid.** "Show me only this" used to leave
  the part at the opacity it has in place, which is right for the opaque ones
  and useless for the see-through ones — and a structure you cannot see in
  place is exactly the one a reader isolates. Nothing else is drawn, so there
  is nothing left for it to be transparent against.

## 7. What it must never be used for

Diagnosis, treatment selection, dose selection, prognosis, or planning any
procedure — splenectomy, partial splenectomy or embolisation included.
