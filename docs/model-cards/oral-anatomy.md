## 1. What question this model answers

**Why is a tongue two organs, and where does saliva actually come out?**

## 2. What it is

A mouth seen from in front, drawn open. The **lips** frame the opening; the
**hard palate** vaults over it and the **soft palate** carries on backwards to
the doorway, framed by the two **palatoglossal arches** with a **palatine
tonsil** in the bed behind each. The **upper** and **lower teeth** stand in two
arches and the **mandible** carries the lower one. Between them the tongue, in
its two parts — **oral part** and **root** — meeting at the line the **vallate
papillae** lie along, with the **lingual tonsil** behind it. Under the tongue,
the **floor of the mouth** and the **lingual frenulum**; outside it, the
**sublingual**, **submandibular** and **parotid** glands, with the
**submandibular** and **parotid ducts** drawn to the points they open at. **19
structures are selectable.**

## 3. What it is not

- **The jaw is drawn open by a display amount** (`JAW_DISPLAY_OPENING`), because
  a closed mouth shows nothing at all. It is a **position** rather than a change
  of shape — nothing is stretched or resized — but **no measurement of opening,
  and no occlusal relationship, may be read off the model.**
- **Nothing moves.** Nothing is chewed or swallowed, the tongue does not move,
  the soft palate does not lift, the jaw does not close and no saliva flows.
- **Teeth are drawn as bands following the arch.** **No individual tooth, root,
  crown shape, number or eruption stage is represented**, and this model must
  not be used for anything dental.
- **The ducts are drawn to the points they open at, not as openings.** The
  caruncle and the parotid papilla are described in words.
- **Nine vallate papillae stand for eight to twelve**, and no other papillae are
  drawn. The foramen cecum is not drawn.
- **No length, angle or gland volume is a measurement.**
- **Not present:** the muscles of the tongue, the floor and the jaw; the joint of
  the jaw and its condyle; the lingual, hypoglossal and facial nerves; the
  vessels; the cheeks; the palatopharyngeal arch; and the midline septum of the
  tongue.
- **No disease**, and not anyone's mouth.

## 4. Sources and licence

Generated by this repository. No external asset, no imaging, no patient data.

## 5. Accuracy and uncertainty

The model makes two claims and the tests fix both. **The tongue is two parts**:
they meet at `SULCUS_Z`, they do not overlap, the row of vallate papillae lies
on that line, and nothing else on the surface marks it — the lingual tonsil is
behind it, on the root. **A duct opens nowhere near its gland**: each duct starts
inside its own gland and ends at the point its copy names; the parotid's opening
is level with the upper teeth while its gland is behind the jaw, and the
submandibular's opening is beside the frenulum while its gland hangs under the
jaw. The tests also fix the frame the rest of it hangs on — the tongue between
the palate above and the floor below, and the tonsil behind the arch.

## 6. Presentation choices

- **The scene opens on the view a mouth is looked at from**: from in front, into
  an open one. Every other view is a step from there.
- **The slider fades the jaw and the tongue**, which is what the floor and the
  glands are behind. Nothing is moved out from under anything.
- **The vallate papillae do not fade with the tongue**, because they are the
  only mark of the boundary the tongue's two parts are a claim about.
- **One arch, everything on it.** `archHalfWidth(z)` is where the dental arch
  is; the two rows of teeth, the mandible under them and the floor slung between
  them all follow it, so they cannot disagree
  (`docs/architecture-rules.md` rule 1).
- **The mandible is drawn with its rami.** Without them a mandible is a
  horseshoe, and a horseshoe is not what anybody recognises as a jaw — and the
  parotid gland would have nothing to lie against.
- **Screen-left is the patient's right** (`docs/architecture-rules.md` rule 5);
  every paired structure comes from one `LEFT` constant.

## 7. What it must never be used for

Diagnosis, treatment selection, dose selection, prognosis, or planning any
procedure — **including anything dental**, which this model is explicitly
incapable of supporting: it draws two bands where there are thirty-two teeth.
