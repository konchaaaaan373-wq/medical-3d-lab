## 1. What question this model answers

**What connects to what, in what order, from the testis to the outside?**

## 2. What it is

One continuous route: **testis** → **epididymis** → **vas deferens** →
(**seminal vesicle** joins) → **ejaculatory duct** → **prostatic urethra** →
**membranous urethra** → **spongy urethra** → **external urethral orifice**.
The **prostate** it passes through and the three **erectile columns** the last
stretch runs inside are drawn around it, with the **bladder** where the urinary
route begins. **14 structures are selectable.**

Each segment's curve starts where the last one ends, read from the curve rather
than typed a second time, so the chain cannot be broken by moving a piece of it.

## 3. What it is not

- **Not measured.** Lengths and calibres are drawn to be legible. The **vas is
  drawn far shorter and straighter than it is** — a real one is about 45 cm,
  most of it coiled — and the epididymal duct inside the epididymis, several
  metres of it, is not modelled at all.
- **The epididymis is one structure.** Head, body and tail are drawn as three
  lengths of one tube but are not separately selectable.
- **One side of a paired route.** The other testis, epididymis, vas and vesicle
  are not drawn.
- **The prostate is one undivided gland here.** Its four zones, the
  verumontanum and the ejaculatory ducts inside it are `prostate-anatomy`;
  drawing them twice would be two prostates.
- **Not present:** the scrotum, the spermatic cord's coverings and vessels, the
  seminiferous tubules and rete testis, the sphincters, the bulbourethral
  glands, the navicular fossa, the erectile mechanism.
- **Nothing changes state**, and nothing flows.

## 4. Sources and licence

Generated by this repository. No external asset, no imaging, no patient data.

## 5. Accuracy and uncertainty

The claim is the **order and continuity of the route**, and that is what the
test fixes: every consecutive pair of segments meets, from the testis to the
spongy urethra; the epididymis lies on the posterior side of the testis; the vas
reaches the prostate from behind; the ejaculatory duct ends inside the gland; and
the membranous urethra is the narrowest of the three lengths.

No dimension here is a measurement.

## 6. Presentation choices

- **The prostate and the erectile columns fade rather than leave.** A duct is
  only "inside a gland" while the gland is there, and the whole point of the
  ejaculatory duct's position is that it is inside one.
- **Three viewpoints follow the route** — the whole of it, the testis end, and
  the junction where the genital route meets the urinary one.
- **The prostate and the columns are context**, excluded from the framing
  subject, so the route is what the frame is set on.
- **Colour runs along the route**; "Natural tissue" puts one set of tissue
  colours back.
- **The frame follows the viewpoint.** The camera fits the organ into the part
  of the window nothing is covering — the parts panel is an overlay over the
  canvas, not a narrower canvas — and a viewpoint that hides a side or cuts the
  organ open is framed on what it leaves rather than on the whole model.

## 7. What it must never be used for

Diagnosis, treatment selection, dose selection, prognosis, or planning any
procedure — vasectomy, catheterisation, surgery or any fertility decision
included.
