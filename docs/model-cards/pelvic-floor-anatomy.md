## 1. What question this model answers

**What is the pelvic floor actually shaped like, and what goes through it?**

## 2. What it is

A female pelvic floor in its frame. The **pelvic ring**, **sacrum** and
**coccyx** are the frame; the **obturator internus** lines its side wall and the
**tendinous arch** on that wall is where the sheet hangs from. The sheet itself
is named in three slices front to back — **pubococcygeus**, **iliococcygeus**,
**coccygeus** — and its two sides stop short of the midline in front, so the
**urogenital hiatus** between them is a real gap. The **puborectalis** is the
one part that is not a sheet: a sling passing behind the bowel. Below, the
**perineal body**, the **perineal membrane** and the **external anal
sphincter**. Through it: the **urethra** and the **vagina** in the gap, and the
**rectum** and **anal canal** behind the sling. **17 structures are
selectable.**

## 3. What it is not

- **The bones are a frame, not a model of bones.** There is no iliac wing, no
  acetabulum and no obturator foramen; the two hip bones and the symphysis
  between them are not separated.
- **Nothing contracts and nothing descends.** No muscle shortens, no pressure is
  represented, and **the anorectal angle the sling exists to make is drawn at
  one fixed value that is not a measurement.**
- **No length, angle, thickness or bone dimension is a measurement.**
- **Only a female pelvis is drawn.**
- **Not present:** the named subdivisions of the levator, the fascia and the
  ligaments that suspend the viscera, the pudendal nerve, the vessels, the
  internal anal sphincter, and the bladder and uterus above.
- **No disease**, and not anyone's pelvis. **Nothing in this scene supports any
  claim about childbirth, continence, prolapse or any procedure.**

## 4. Sources and licence

Generated by this repository. No external asset, no imaging, no patient data.

## 5. Accuracy and uncertainty

The model claims an arrangement, and the tests fix it: the three slices of the
sheet run front to back without overlapping and all hang from the same origin
line the tendinous arch is drawn along; the two sides stop short of the midline
in front, so the hiatus between them is bounded by the sheet's own medial edges;
the urethra and the vagina pass through that gap and the bowel does not; the
puborectalis passes behind the anorectal junction; and the perineal body lies
between the vagina in front and the anal canal behind.

## 6. Presentation choices

- **The slider fades the bone**, because the bone is the frame the sheet is
  inside. Nothing is moved out of the ring.
- **Two viewpoints carry the subject and neither works flat**: from above, the
  gap in the sheet is a hole you can see through; from below, the sling reads as
  a sling. A drawing can have one or the other.
- **The hiatus is drawn as the space it is**, faint because it is an absence and
  solid enough to be picked because it is the scene's answer.
- **One pair of functions, five uses.** `levatorOrigin` and `levatorInsertion`
  are the two lines the sheet is stretched between; every named part of the
  levator, and the tendinous arch it hangs from, is a slice between them
  (`docs/architecture-rules.md` rule 1).
- **Screen-left is the patient's right** (`docs/architecture-rules.md` rule 5);
  every paired structure comes from one `LEFT` constant.

## 7. What it must never be used for

Diagnosis, treatment selection, dose selection, prognosis, or planning any
procedure — and, specifically, **anything about childbirth, continence or
prolapse**, all of which are about a floor that moves, and nothing here moves.
