## 1. What question this model answers

**Where is the oesophagus narrow, and what makes it narrow there?**

## 2. What it is

One tube on a path, cut into **cervical**, **thoracic** and **abdominal**
parts, with three narrowings built into its calibre and a ring marking each at
the profile's own minimum: the **cricopharyngeal**, the **aortic and
bronchial**, and the **diaphragmatic**. Beside them are the structures that make
each one — the **trachea**, the **aortic arch**, the **left main bronchus**, the
**diaphragm** it passes through, and the **cardia** it ends at. **11 structures
are selectable.**

The slider brings the neighbours up rather than fading the tube: here the tube
is the subject from the first frame, and what the slider adds is the reason.

## 3. What it is not

- **Not measured.** Lengths, calibres and angles are drawn to be legible.
  **No distance from the incisors is given or implied**, and none may be read
  off this model.
- **The middle constriction is drawn as one.** It is often counted as two, the
  aortic and the bronchial; both structures that make it are separately
  selectable here.
- **Not present:** muscle layers and the change from striated to smooth, the
  upper and lower oesophageal sphincters, the diaphragmatic crura, the vagus
  nerves, the submucosal venous plexus. Nothing here shows why reflux is
  resisted, or how varices form.
- **No swallowing.** Nothing moves; `upper-gi-peristalsis` is where that lives.

## 4. Sources and licence

Generated by this repository. No external asset, no imaging, no patient data.

## 5. Accuracy and uncertainty

The claim is the **order of the parts** and **which structure makes each
narrowing**. Both are held in `tests/organ-parts-anatomy.test.js`: the three
parts run in order and cover the tube once; each ring sits at a minimum of the
same calibre profile the tube is built from; the arch is behind and the bronchus
in front at the middle narrowing; the diaphragm surrounds the lowest one.

No dimension here is a measurement.

## 6. Presentation choices

- **The rings are placed at the profile's minima**, not at three numbers typed
  beside it — a ring placed by hand would drift the moment the profile changed.
- **Two close-up viewpoints exist**, at the crossing and at the hiatus, because
  both relations are small relative to the whole tube.
- **The neighbours are context**, excluded from the framing subject.
- **Colour separates the three parts and the three narrowings**; "Natural
  tissue" puts one mucosal colour back.
- **Taking a structure out of the way is the reader's own way in.** The layer
  slider, and where this scene has them the cuts and the viewpoints, are its
  *authored* ways of showing what is inside; hiding is the one the reader
  chooses — one structure, or a whole branch of the part tree in a single
  press. It asserts nothing anatomical and moves nothing: a hidden structure is
  still there, still listed, still findable by name, and **Unhide all** brings
  every one of them back. While it is off screen it also leaves the picker, so
  a ray cannot select what the reader cannot see. Hiding the structure that is
  currently isolated ends the isolation, because "only this one" and "not this
  one" cannot both be true, and that ending is announced rather than left for a
  panel to notice.

## 7. What it must never be used for

Diagnosis, treatment selection, dose selection, prognosis, or planning any
procedure — endoscopy, dilatation, stenting or foreign-body removal included.
