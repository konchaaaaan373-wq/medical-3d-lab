## 1. What question this model answers

**How deep is a thing in skin, and which way does it reach the surface?**

## 2. What it is

A **block of skin with its sides cut** — a specimen, not a body part with a
shape. **Epidermis**, **dermis** and **subcutaneous tissue** stack, each layer's
floor being the next one's roof. **Adipose tissue** fills the subcutaneous
compartment as lobules, so the compartment and its contents can each be pointed
at. A **hair follicle** runs from the surface down into the fat, carrying the
hair it makes; a **sebaceous gland** opens into it. A **sweat gland** takes the
other route, with a duct that opens on the surface away from any hair. An
**arteriole**, a **venule** and a **sensory nerve** run in the dermis and stop
below the epidermis. **10 structures are selectable.**

## 3. What it is not

- **The layers are deliberately not to scale.** The epidermis is drawn at about
  a quarter of the dermis where in life it is about a twentieth, so that what is
  in it can be seen. It is a display thickness (`LAYER_DISPLAY_THICKNESS`), and
  **no thickness or ratio may be read off this model**.
- **Nothing moves or grows.** No shedding, no hair cycle, no sweating, no
  healing.
- **One block stands for all skin.** Palm, scalp, eyelid and back are not
  distinguished, and they differ more than this model can say.
- **Not present:** the layers of the epidermis itself, the arrector pili muscle,
  the named sensory receptors, the lymphatics, the fibrous septa between fat
  lobules.
- **One arteriole and one venule** stand for two plexuses that are not drawn
  separately.
- **No disease**, and not anyone's skin.

## 4. Sources and licence

Generated by this repository. No external asset, no imaging, no patient data.

## 5. Accuracy and uncertainty

What the model claims is **depth and route**, and that is what the tests fix:
the three layers stack in order and meet without gaps; the dermo-epidermal
junction interlocks rather than lying flat; the follicle starts above the
surface and ends below the dermis; the sebaceous gland touches the follicle and
never reaches the surface; the sweat duct reaches the surface and opens well
away from the hair; neither vessel nor nerve enters the epidermis; and every
fat lobule is inside the subcutaneous compartment.

## 6. Presentation choices

- **The block is already cut**, so the section is what the model is rather than
  a viewpoint on it, and nothing has to be taken away to see inside.
- **A "surface" view exists as well as a cut one.** Skin is the one organ a
  reader has already seen; starting from the face they know is what makes the
  cut mean anything.
- **The slider fades the layers**, which is what the follicle, the glands and
  the vessels are inside.
- **The fat is drawn as lobules inside the compartment**, not as the compartment
  itself, so a reader can point at either.
- **The follicle and its hair are one structure**, because a hair is what a
  follicle produces and not a separate organ.

## 7. What it must never be used for

Diagnosis, treatment selection, dose selection, prognosis, staging a wound, or
planning any procedure. It carries no depth in millimetres and no threshold.
