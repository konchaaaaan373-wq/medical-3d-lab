## 1. What question this model answers

**Where is a limb actually attached to the trunk?**

## 2. What it is

A whole standing skeleton at the scale where the question is how it is put
together. The column: **skull**, **mandible**, **cervical**, **thoracic** and
**lumbar spine**, **sacrum and coccyx**, with the **ribs** and **sternum**
making a cage on it. The shoulder girdle: **clavicle** and **scapula**. The arm:
**humerus**, **radius and ulna**, **hand bones**. The pelvic girdle: the **hip
bones**. The leg: **femur**, **patella**, **tibia and fibula**, **foot bones**.
**18 structures are selectable.**

## 3. What it is not

- **No bone here is a model of that bone.** Every one is a smooth shaft or block
  with no joint surfaces, no processes, no sutures and no foramina; the
  vertebrae, the carpal and tarsal bones and the bones of the hands and feet are
  **not drawn individually at all**.
- **This is the map, not the territory.** The scenes that do model a region —
  `spine-anatomy`, `shoulder-anatomy`, `hip-anatomy`, `knee-anatomy`,
  `hand-anatomy`, `foot-anatomy`, `pelvic-floor-anatomy`, `brain-anatomy` — are
  separate, and each structure's note names the one a reader should go to.
- **Nothing moves and nothing bears weight.**
- **No bone length, proportion, joint angle or bone count is a measurement.** The
  figure is one representative adult of **no particular sex, age or size**.
- **Not present:** cartilage, ligaments, muscle, marrow and every soft tissue.
- **No disease**, and not anyone's skeleton.

## 4. Sources and licence

Generated by this repository. No external asset, no imaging, no patient data.

## 5. Accuracy and uncertainty

What the model claims is an **arrangement**, and the tests fix it: the column
runs continuously from the skull to the sacrum in order with no gaps; the
clavicle reaches the sternum while the scapula touches no other bone at all; the
hip bone reaches the sacrum on both sides; the ribs run from the thoracic spine
forwards with the upper ones reaching the sternum and the lower ones stopping
short; and every joint of a limb is below the one above it. Nothing about the
shape of any bone is claimed.

## 6. Presentation choices

- **The slider fades the limbs**, which leaves the column and the two girdles
  standing on it — the contrast the scene is about. Nothing is detached from
  anything.
- **The two girdles fade last and least**, because they are the join.
- **Each structure's note sends the reader somewhere else.** That is a
  deliberate design choice for an overview: the alternative is a scene that
  looks authoritative about bones it draws as cylinders.
- **One table, every bone.** `LEVELS` is the figure's proportions and nothing in
  the file places itself at a number not derived from it, so the figure cannot
  end up with an elbow above its shoulder
  (`docs/architecture-rules.md` rule 1). `spineAt(y)` is the curve of the
  column, used by the spine, the sacrum and every rib.
- **Screen-left is the patient's right** (`docs/architecture-rules.md` rule 5);
  every paired structure comes from one `LEFT` constant.

## 7. What it must never be used for

Diagnosis, treatment selection, dose selection, prognosis, or planning any
procedure — and, specifically, **anything about the shape of a bone**, which
this model does not attempt and is not capable of.
