## 1. What question this model answers

**Two pleural cavities take almost the whole chest. Where does everything else
go?**

## 2. What it is

A **region**, not an organ. The **manubrium**, **body of the sternum** and
**xiphoid process**; the **ribs**, the **costal cartilages** and the **thoracic
vertebrae**; the **intercostal spaces** and the **vessels and nerve** under each
rib; the **diaphragm** under all of it. Then the **parietal pleura** lining each
cavity, the **costodiaphragmatic recess** no lung reaches, and the **right** and
**left lungs** in them. Between the two cavities, the **mediastinum** as a
space, with the **heart** in its **pericardium**, the **trachea and main
bronchi**, and the **oesophagus**. Crossing it, the **aorta**, the **venae
cavae**, the **pulmonary arteries**, and the **phrenic** and **vagus nerves**.
**23 structures are selectable.**

## 3. What it is not

- **Nothing moves.** Nothing breathes, the ribs do not rise, the diaphragm does
  not descend and the heart does not beat. **No pressure, volume or compliance
  exists anywhere in this model**, and the size of the recess is not a capacity.
- **The lungs are silhouettes in their place, not an atlas.** The lobes and
  fissures are named and not separated; the twenty bronchopulmonary segments are
  in `lung-anatomy`, which is a different scale and a different question.
- **The heart is an outline in its place.** No chamber, valve, coronary vessel
  or wall. `cardiovascular/organs/heart.js` is the landmark outline used
  elsewhere and the heart-failure scene has one generated from a haemodynamic
  model; this is neither.
- **The diaphragm's three openings are described, not drawn as openings.** What
  is drawn is the structures crossing it and the order of the levels they cross
  at — caval highest, oesophageal next, aortic lowest and behind rather than
  through.
- **The intercostal bundle is one cord on the usual course.** In life the order
  is not always kept, the bundle is less sheltered posteriorly, and there are
  collateral branches along the lower border of the space.
- **No dimension, calibre, angle, rib spacing or volume is a measurement.**
- **Not present:** the three layers of intercostal muscle, the visceral pleura
  and the film of fluid between the layers — which is what actually holds a lung
  out against the wall — the azygos vein, the pulmonary veins, every branch of
  the aorta, the bronchial tree below the main bronchi, the thoracic duct, the
  sympathetic chain, the thymus, the breast and the muscles of the chest wall.
- **No disease**, and not anyone's chest.

## 4. Sources and licence

Generated by this repository. No external asset, no imaging, no patient data.

## 5. Accuracy and uncertainty

What the model claims is **the arrangement**, and that is what the tests fix:
every rib ends lower at the front than it starts at the back and further
forward, having gone round the side; the upper costal cartilages reach the
sternum; the neurovascular bundle lies below its own rib and above the next one
down; **no part of either lung lies inside the mediastinum, and the left lung is
held further from the midline than the right** — its cardiac notch; each lung's
base stops above the floor of its own cavity while the recess goes below it; the
right main bronchus is wider, shorter and more upright than the left; **at each
hilum the phrenic nerve passes in front and the vagus behind**; and the
oesophagus lies behind the airway at every level and behind the heart.

## 6. Presentation choices

- **The slider takes away the wall** — the cage first, then the spaces and the
  pleura behind them — because that is the only part of a chest that can be
  removed without moving anything. Nothing inside is moved.
- **The recess does not fade with the pleura**, because it is the answer to one
  of the scene's questions and a reader looking for it needs it all the way
  down.
- **A rib is a run round the chest, not a drawn curve.** `chestSection(y)` is
  the outline at each height and `ribPath` sweeps round it, so the slope that
  makes a cage a cage is one number per level and the cartilages continue on the
  same run (`docs/architecture-rules.md` rule 1).
- **The cardiac notch is not drawn.** Both lungs are built filling their side of
  the chest and then pressed out of `mediastinumSection` by `clearMediastinum`,
  so the notch **is** the mediastinum's boundary and cannot drift from what is
  inside it. The lung is pushed to its own side, not to whichever side of the
  slab's centre it started on — sorting by the centre put half a left lung on
  the right of the chest.
- **The two nerves are placed from the same hilum point**, one in front of it
  and one behind, so the single relationship that separates them cannot be lost.
- **Screen-left is the patient's right** (`docs/architecture-rules.md` rule 5);
  every paired structure comes from one `LEFT` constant.
- **Laid out in centimetres and scaled once** (`WORLD_SCALE`), as the neck, the
  skeleton and the foot are, because the shared viewer clamps the camera
  (`docs/follow-ups.md` F-90).
- **The frame follows the viewpoint.** The camera fits the organ into the part
  of the window nothing is covering — the parts panel is an overlay over the
  canvas, not a narrower canvas — and a viewpoint that hides a side or cuts the
  organ open is framed on what it leaves rather than on the whole model.
- **A cut draws the face it leaves, where there is one to draw.** For a solid
  part the cross-section is computed from the triangles the plane crosses and
  drawn in that part's own colour, so a cut segment reads as tissue with a
  surface rather than as a shell seen from the inside. For a hollow one — a
  stomach, a duct, a loop of bowel — the cut opens it instead: the wall here is
  a surface with no thickness, and facing it would draw a lumen as a lump of
  tissue. Which a part is, is declared by the scene rather than guessed.

## 7. What it must never be used for

Diagnosis, treatment selection, dose selection, prognosis, counting ribs or
spaces on an image, or planning or performing any **puncture, drain, line,
compression, incision or operation**. The spaces, the recess and the bundle are
drawn because they are landmarks worth being able to point at, not because this
model knows anything about reaching them.
