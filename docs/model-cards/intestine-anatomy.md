## 1. What question this model answers

**Where does each named part of the colon run, and what is the frame it makes
around the small bowel?**

## 2. What it is

The colon is drawn as one tube on a fixed frame, with haustra as a periodic
change in calibre. This scene cuts that same frame at its own corners into
**caecum, ascending colon, right colic (hepatic) flexure, transverse colon,
left colic (splenic) flexure, descending colon and sigmoid colon**, and adds
the **small intestine** that fills the middle and the **duodenum**. **9
structures are selectable.**

The boundaries are measured along the path, not assumed from the spacing of its
control points: a Catmull-Rom curve does not place them at even fractions of
its own arc length, and treating index over count as a fraction put the splenic
flexure in the middle of the transverse colon.

## 3. What it is not

- **The small bowel is one structure.** Jejunum and ileum differ in wall,
  calibre and mesentery and shade into one another; nothing in this coil marks a
  boundary, so the scene does not offer one to select.
- **A flexure is a bend.** The two are selectable so a reader can point at the
  corner; each is the short length of tube there, not a segment of colon in its
  own right.
- **Not present at all:** appendix, ileocaecal valve, rectum, anal canal,
  mesentery, taenia coli, appendices epiploicae, wall layers.
- **Where the caecum ends is schematic.** No ileum is drawn arriving, so there
  is nothing in the geometry to measure the junction from.
- **Not a transit model.** Nothing moves; `intestinal-transit` is where motility
  lives.

## 4. Sources and licence

Generated by this repository. No external asset, no imaging, no patient data.

## 5. Accuracy and uncertainty

`tests/organ-anatomy.test.js` holds the arrangement — ascending on the
patient's right and descending on the left — and the part division is held
through the anatomy contract in `tests/organ-anatomy-scenes.test.js`.

Lengths, loop counts and positions are illustrative. The colon's real length,
the number of small-bowel loops and the height of either flexure are not
claims this model makes.

What the model *does* claim about length is the **order**, which
`tests/organ-parts-anatomy.test.js` holds: transverse and sigmoid longest,
then descending, then ascending, then caecum. The ratios are not the ones a
textbook gives — the transverse colon is about 1.1 times the descending here
rather than roughly 2 — because the abdominal frame is fixed and the
descending colon has a whole abdomen to fall down while the transverse only
has one to cross. **Do not read a length or a ratio off this model.**

## 6. Presentation choices

- **The scene says what frame shape it needs.** The colon frame is nearly
  square — the widest view fills the frame's width at an aspect of 0.81 — so a
  narrow window pulls back rather than cropping the flexures.
- **The duodenum's two ends are closed as domes.** Neither is a blind end —
  one continues from the pylorus, one into the jejunum — but a flat disc there
  read as a cut pipe. The dome is where the model stops, not where the bowel
  does.
- **The slider fades the small bowel**, which is what stands in front of the
  colon and hides most of it. Nothing is moved and nothing is removed.
- **A viewpoint takes the small bowel away entirely**, which is different from
  fading it, and is labelled as its own view.
- **Colour separates the parts**; "Natural tissue" puts one bowel colour back.
- **The frame follows the viewpoint.** The camera fits the organ into the part
  of the window nothing is covering — the parts panel is an overlay over the
  canvas, not a narrower canvas — and a viewpoint that hides a side or cuts the
  organ open is framed on what it leaves rather than on the whole model.
- **A cut draws the face it leaves, where there is one to draw.** For a solid
  part the cross-section is computed from the triangles the plane crosses and
  drawn in that part's own colour, so a cut segment reads as tissue with a
  surface rather than as a shell seen from the inside. For a hollow one — a
  stomach, a duct, a loop of bowel — the cut opens it instead: the wall here is
  a surface with no thickness, and facing it would draw a lumen as a lump of
  tissue. Which a part is, is declared by the scene rather than guessed.
- **Isolating a structure shows it solid.** "Show me only this" used to leave
  the part at the opacity it has in place, which is right for the opaque ones
  and useless for the see-through ones — and a structure you cannot see in
  place is exactly the one a reader isolates. Nothing else is drawn, so there
  is nothing left for it to be transparent against.

## 7. What it must never be used for

Diagnosis, treatment selection, dose selection, prognosis, or planning any
procedure — colonoscopy, resection or stoma siting included.
