## 1. What question this model answers

**Which part of the stomach is this, and where does one part stop and the next
begin?**

## 2. What it is

The stomach is drawn as one tube of varying calibre along the greater
curvature, because that is what makes peristalsis expressible and it is what
`upper-gi-peristalsis` uses. This scene cuts that same tube — the same path,
the same calibre — into the lengths anatomy names: **fundus, cardia, body,
pyloric antrum and pyloric canal**, plus the **pyloric sphincter** as its own
ring, the **abdominal oesophagus** that opens in, and the **duodenum** the
pylorus opens into. **8 structures are selectable.**

Where the boundaries fall comes from the model rather than from a second
description of it. The calibre profile is written with the regions named
against it, and the divisions are those. The **cardia** is the exception, and
deliberately: it is not a length of the greater curvature but the place where
the oesophagus opens in, so it is found from where that tube ends. Move the
oesophagus and the cardia moves with it.

## 3. What it is not

- **The parts are rings of a tube.** The cardia is a region on the
  lesser-curvature side of the opening and is drawn here as a collar all the way
  round. The incisura angularis is a notch on the lesser curvature and is
  present only as the narrowing that goes with it.
- **No wall.** No mucosa, submucosa, muscular layers or serosa; no rugae; no
  gastric glands or parietal cells.
- **No volume.** Nothing here says how much the stomach holds, and no emptying
  is modelled: `upper-gi-peristalsis` is where the wave lives.
- **No neighbours** beyond the oesophagus and the duodenum — no diaphragm, no
  spleen, no lesser or greater omentum.

## 4. Sources and licence

Generated by this repository. No external asset, no imaging, no patient data.

## 5. Accuracy and uncertainty

`tests/organ-anatomy.test.js` holds the orientation — a fundus high on the
patient's left running to a pylorus on the right — and the part division is
held by `tests/organ-anatomy-scenes.test.js` through the anatomy contract:
every named part resolves to its own mesh, the tree and the model agree, and
the union of the parts is the stomach that was there before.

What is not held is any dimension. The stomach's proportions are drawn to read
clearly; this scene supports no measurement of any kind.

## 6. Presentation choices

- **The layer slider is a wall transparency**, and is labelled as one. There is
  no second layer inside this stomach; what is behind the wall is the sphincter
  ring and the duodenum, and the slider lets a reader see them through it.
- **Colour separates the five regions.** "Natural tissue" puts one gastric
  colour back on all of them. Neither is measured tissue colour.

## 7. What it must never be used for

Diagnosis, treatment selection, dose selection, prognosis, or planning any
procedure — endoscopy, biopsy, resection or sleeve included.
